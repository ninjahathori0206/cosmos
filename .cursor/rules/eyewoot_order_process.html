Shared order engine: POS, Eyewoot Go, and Army all write to the same orders table. Channel is identified by order_source = POS / APP / ARMY. No separate tables per channel.
Instant order — Sunglasses, zero-power frames, accessories, in-stock contact lenses. Customer takes items in the same visit. No lab involved.
Lab order — Spectacle glasses and out-of-stock contact lenses. Full 9-stage lifecycle from order placement to invoice. Lenses are ALWAYS processed by Foundry HQ — no exceptions.
Mixed order — One parent order ID, two sub-orders. Sub-order A (instant) closes same day. Sub-order B (lab) follows the full 9-stage lifecycle. One invoice is generated covering both after all payments are complete.
Sub-order A — Instant
  • Sunglasses, accessories, in-stock contact lenses
  • Stock deducted at billing — immediately
  • Full payment collected at POS
  • Freebies from store stock — given at counter
  • Customer takes items same visit
  • Sub-order closes same day
Sub-order B — Lab
  • Spectacle glasses, out-of-stock contact lenses
  • Advance payment collected at billing
  • Goes through full 9-stage lab lifecycle
  • Freebies packed by HQ lab
  • Balance collected on delivery / pickup
  • Invoice only after full balance paid
Contact lens split scenario: Customer orders 6 boxes of a specific power. Store has 4 in stock. System automatically creates Sub-order A (4 boxes — instant) + Sub-order B (2 boxes — procurement). Staff sees this split clearly on the POS order summary screen.

Critical rule — invoice only after full payment. For lab orders, no GST invoice is ever generated at advance time. An advance receipt (non-GST slip) is issued to protect both parties. The GST invoice is generated only when the full balance is collected and the order is marked INVOICED.

Procurement mode — controlled by super admin in CommandUnit

Mode A — Store stock: Each store fulfills from its own inventory. Frame stock is deducted immediately on order. Store is both display and fulfillment source. Lenses always from HQ regardless of this setting.
Order placed at store
→
Store inventory checked for stock availability
Stock deducted
→
Store stock reduced immediately on order creation — not at fulfillment
Frame sent to lab
→
Physical store display frame dispatched to Foundry HQ lab for lens fitting
Mode B — HQ stock: Stores become display-only. All frame fulfillment from Foundry HQ. Store stock is never deducted. HQ stock is reserved at order time and deducted only when physically dispatched from HQ.
Order placed
→
HQ Foundry stock checked for availability across warehouse
HQ stock reserved
→
Frame reserved at HQ (status = ONLINE_RESERVED). Prevents double-selling across concurrent orders.
HQ dispatches to lab
→
HQ sends frame to Foundry lab. Stock deducted from HQ at dispatch — not at order time.
Reserve vs deduct: In Mode B, stock is reserved at order time but deducted only at dispatch. This prevents two POS terminals simultaneously selling the last HQ unit.
