POS Settings
Lab advance (% of lab-portion catalogue value), GST mode (composition / inclusive catalogue), and default POS GST rate.
Turn off when you exit composition; GST is then computed from catalogue rate and inclusivity below.
Offers/discount apply on the GST-inclusive cart subtotal before tax is backed out when GST applies.
Ignored on the bill while composition is on; kept for when you disable composition.
Applies to all stores. Can be adjusted later by policy updates.

Store OS tablets pick up GST and composition changes when they next open the cart, return to this browser tab, or reload — no separate POS “GST” screen on checkout.
